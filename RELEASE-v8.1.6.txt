Release v8.2.3 — Cross-Device Cloud Vault Locator

- Publishes a private per-user Vault locator after every successful encrypted sync.
- New iPhone/iPad devices resolve the canonical trip with one direct Firestore read.
- Discovery no longer depends on stale localStorage, guessed trip IDs, or a potentially hanging trip-list query.
- Firestore bridge recovery can be discovered even when Firebase Storage is unavailable.
- Adds the required authenticated-owner rule for users/{uid}/vaultLocator/{documentId}.
