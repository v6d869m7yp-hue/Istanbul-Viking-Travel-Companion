Photography guide

The most effective photographs show scale: water in the foreground, old settlements in the middle distance and mountains behind.

Bay panorama

Use a wide view but include a boat, roofline or church to provide scale.

Perast

Look down the waterfront rather than shooting every building straight-on.

Old Town

Use doorways and archways as frames for lanes and squares.

Heat and glare

Expose for pale stone and bright water; shadows can be lifted later.
