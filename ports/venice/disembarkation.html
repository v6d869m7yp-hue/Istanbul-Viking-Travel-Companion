Disembarkation & hotel transfer

The best transfer is the one that minimizes bridge crossings with luggage. Confirm the exact terminal and Viking transfer instructions in the final Viking Daily.

  1. Night beforePack documents, medication and valuables in carry-on luggage.
  2. MorningBreakfast, cabin sweep and luggage-color confirmation.
  3. TerminalCollect bags and verify the exact onward transfer point.
  4. Venice arrivalFavor a water taxi or a route with the fewest bridges if luggage is heavy.
  5. HotelStore bags if the room is not yet ready, then begin with a light neighborhood walk.
Important: The companion intentionally leaves the final terminal-to-hotel leg flexible because cruise docking and transfer arrangements can change. Confirm the exact route onboard.
