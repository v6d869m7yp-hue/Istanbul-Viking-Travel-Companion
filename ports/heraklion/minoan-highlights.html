Minoan highlights

“Minoan” is a modern label for the Bronze Age civilization centered on Crete. The museum gives the material evidence; the myths of Minos, the labyrinth and the Minotaur came later.

c. 2000 BC
First palaces
Large administrative and ceremonial centers develop.
c. 1700 BC
Palace rebuilding
Knossos and other centers reach their most elaborate form.
c. 1450 BC
Major disruption
Many palace centers are destroyed; Mycenaean influence increases.
Evidence

What archaeology shows

Palaces, storage systems, frescoes, seal stones, ritual objects, trade and two writing systems.

Tradition

What myth adds

King Minos, the labyrinth and the Minotaur provide cultural memory, not a literal excavation report.

Look for

  • Double-axe motifs and horns of consecration
  • Sea creatures used as decoration
  • Linear A inscriptions that remain undeciphered
  • Fresco fragments reconstructed from small surviving pieces
