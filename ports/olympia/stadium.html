The ancient stadium

Visitor sequence

  1. Approach through the vaulted passage used by athletes and officials.
  2. Pause at the stone starting line.
  3. Look back toward the sanctuary to understand the connection between worship and competition.
  4. Use the earthen banks to estimate the scale of the crowd.
Photography

Use a person near the starting line for scale. A wide view from one side of the track communicates the stadium better than a straight-on image.
