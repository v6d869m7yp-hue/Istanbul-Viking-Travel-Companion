How to see Olympia

Think in three layers: the sacred sanctuary, the athletic stadium and the museum objects that restore scale and human detail. The ruins can appear low and fragmented, so the route and reconstruction context matter.

Travel intelligence

Do not use all your energy photographing every foundation. Prioritize the Temple of Zeus, the stadium entrance and the museum.

Look for

  • The scale of fallen column drums at the Temple of Zeus.
  • The curved starting line and long earthen banks of the stadium.
  • The contrast between the site’s quiet landscape and the Games’ historic importance.
