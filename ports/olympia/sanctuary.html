The Sanctuary chapters

The sacred Altis

Olympia’s core was a sanctuary, not merely an athletic venue. The Temple of Zeus anchored the complex and once housed the colossal gold-and-ivory statue counted among the Seven Wonders.

What to notice

Temple of Zeus

Massive column drums, Doric proportions and the platform that organized the sanctuary’s most important cult building.

Heraion

Older and more intimate; the modern Olympic flame ceremony is associated with this area.

Philippeion

A circular memorial associated with Philip II and the Macedonian dynasty.

Workshop of Pheidias

Linked to the creation of the monumental statue of Zeus.
