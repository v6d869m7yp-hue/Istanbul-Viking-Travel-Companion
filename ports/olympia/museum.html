Museum priorities

The museum supplies the artistic detail missing from the ruined sanctuary. If time is tight, concentrate on three groups.

Three priorities

  1. Temple of Zeus pediments: read them as monumental storytelling designed for the temple exterior.
  2. Hermes: circle the sculpture and notice the changing silhouette.
  3. Nike: imagine the original elevated placement and wind-filled drapery.
