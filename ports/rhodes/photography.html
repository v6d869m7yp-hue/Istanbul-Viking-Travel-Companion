Rhodes photography guide

Rhodes rewards contrast: honey-colored stone in the Old Town, deep blue water at Mandraki and white architecture at Lindos.

SceneBest approach
Palace façadeWide view, slightly off-center, include foreground.
Street of the KnightsShoot uphill, use repeating walls and shadows.
MandrakiInclude boats or windmills for scale and context.
LindosUse a wide frame to show village, acropolis and bay together.
