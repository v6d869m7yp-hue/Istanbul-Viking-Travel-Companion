Palace of the Grand Master

Treat the Palace as a fortified headquarters rather than a decorative royal residence. Its scale, towers and controlled approach explain its power better than any single interior room.

Visitor orientation

Efficient palace sequence

Pause outside. Photograph the complete façade before entering.
Cross the courtyard slowly. This is the best place to feel the scale.
Prioritize context. Interior rooms can blur together; focus on the restored medieval narrative.
Exit toward the knightly quarter. The Street of the Knights makes the architecture legible.

Photography strategy: A wide view from slightly off-center avoids a flat, symmetrical snapshot. In the courtyard, use people for scale.
