What the drive adds

The excursion gives geographic context that the Old Town cannot: the length of the island, the dry August landscape, resort beaches and the whitewashed settlement of Lindos beneath its acropolis.

Pacing: Do not spend the noon interval wandering in full sun. Lunch, hydrate, and return to the meeting point early.
