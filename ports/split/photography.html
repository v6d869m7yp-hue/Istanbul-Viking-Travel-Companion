Photography guide

Split is strongest when you show layers: Roman stone, medieval additions and contemporary life in the same frame.

Peristyle

Use columns to frame the opposite side rather than attempting one very wide shot.

Substructures

Stabilize the camera and expose for highlights near openings.

Riva
