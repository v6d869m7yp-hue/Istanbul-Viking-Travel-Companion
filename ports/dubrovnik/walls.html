City Walls decision guide

A full circuit is memorable but exposed, stepped and time-consuming. Do not add it automatically after a three-hour walking excursion.

Choose the walls

If the day is not excessively hot, your legs feel good and you have a comfortable time margin.

Skip the full circuit

If the Old Town walk already feels tiring, if queues are long or if you want lunch and harbor time.

Best views

Terracotta roofs, the sea-facing walls, Fort Lovrijenac and the Old Port.

Alternative

A short viewpoint near the gates or harbor provides context without committing to the entire circuit.
