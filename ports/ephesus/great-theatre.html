The Great Theatre

The Theatre demonstrates the city’s scale better than almost any single ruin. From the lower level, look through the stage area toward the former harbor axis and imagine the urban approach filled with people.

Scale and city planning

Do not climb automatically

The lower seating already reveals the curve, stage building and Harbor Street alignment. Climb only if the heat, time and your knees make it worthwhile.

What to notice

The steep seating bowl uses the hillside to support enormous capacity.
The stage building once framed performances more fully than today.
Harbor Street aligns the Theatre with the ancient port.
Acoustics remain striking even from the lower rows.

Photography

Use a wide horizontal frame from one lower side rather than standing dead center. A person on the floor or lower seats helps communicate scale.

Energy-saving choice: if the group continues upward, remain at the lower level only if the guide confirms the next meeting point and timing.
