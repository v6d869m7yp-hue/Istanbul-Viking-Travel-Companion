Three decisions to make

Terrace Houses?

Choose them for mosaics and interior life; skip if stair fatigue or tour timing is tight.

Theatre climb?

The lower seating and entry axis convey the scale without climbing high.

Photography?

Take the wide Library photograph first, then details; groups can fill the plaza quickly.

Shopping?

Protect the archaeological visit first. Shopping can usually wait until Kuşadası.
