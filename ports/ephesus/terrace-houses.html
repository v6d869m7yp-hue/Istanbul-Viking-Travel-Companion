The Terrace Houses

These elite residences reveal a side of Ephesus that monumental streets cannot: domestic rooms, mosaics, painted walls, plumbing and carefully organized hillside living.

Is it worth adding?

Choose it when…Skip it when…
You value mosaics, frescoes and daily Roman life.Your tour provides little independent time.
You welcome a covered break from the sun.Stairs or enclosed walkways are tiring.
You are comfortable moving efficiently.The Library and Theatre are higher priorities.

Look for

Black-and-white floor mosaics defining individual rooms.
Painted wall panels imitating richer materials and architecture.
Changes in floor level showing how villas stepped down the slope.
Water and drainage systems supporting elite domestic life.
Tour reality: confirm whether your Viking excursion includes admission and enough time. Do not leave the group without a clear meeting instruction.
