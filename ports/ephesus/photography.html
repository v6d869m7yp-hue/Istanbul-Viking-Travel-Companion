Ephesus chapters

A six-image Ephesus story

Rather than photographing every stone, build a concise sequence that captures city, monument, domestic life and scale.

1 · Curetes Street overview

Show the descending marble street with the Library anchoring the distance.

2 · Architectural detail

Choose one capital, carved frieze or inscription rather than many similar fragments.

3 · Terrace Houses interior

Use the covered light for a mosaic or fresco detail if entry is included.

4 · Library full façade

Make the essential centered image before the plaza becomes crowded.

5 · Library close detail

Frame columns, niches and sculpture vertically.

6 · Theatre scale

Include a person to show the size of the seating bowl.

Phone tip: reduce exposure slightly on pale marble in strong sun. This protects detail in columns and paving that can otherwise turn white.
