Panoramic island drive

Use the coach portion to understand Corfu’s topography: a long, green island with steep coastal folds and villages set above coves.

From the coach

  • Sit on the side with the clearest coastal views if your guide indicates one.
  • Photograph through glass only when the bus is stopped; reflections are strongest in motion.
  • Keep your phone ready but do not let every bend become a photo task.
Travel intelligence

The scenic portion may vary with traffic and operator routing. Treat the map as orientation, not a guaranteed turn-by-turn itinerary.
