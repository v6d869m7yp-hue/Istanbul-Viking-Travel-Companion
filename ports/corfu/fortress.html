The Old Fortress

Should you climb?

If free time is short or the day is hot, enjoy the exterior profile and lower viewpoints. A fuller climb is more rewarding when you have at least 45–60 minutes and are comfortable with slopes and exposed sun.

Best for

Harbor panoramas, defensive architecture and wide views over the town.

Skip or shorten if

You are saving energy, wearing poor footwear or have limited time before the group meeting point.
