Corfu Old Town

A compact fortified town shaped by centuries of Venetian rule, later French and British influence, and daily Greek island life.

Walking priorities

  1. Spianada: orient yourself between the Old Town and fortress.
  2. Liston: use the arcades for shade and a short café pause.
  3. Narrow lanes: look upward at shutters, laundry, balconies and small churches.
  4. Saint Spyridon area: note the town’s religious center and distinctive bell tower.
