Museum strategy

Choose eras, not rooms

Prioritize the Mycenaean world, Archaic sculpture and a few Classical masterpieces. A selective route is more memorable than visual overload.

Connect to Troy

Mycenaean objects help frame the Bronze Age world behind later Greek epic tradition.

Connect to the Acropolis

Archaic and Classical sculpture reveal the artistic language behind Athens’ monuments.
