Day 1: Acropolis & Plaka

Treat the Acropolis as an exposed ascent rather than a conventional museum visit. The goal is steady pacing, careful footing and enough attention left to appreciate the monuments.

Before leaving the ship

Carry light

Water, hat, sunscreen and shoes with reliable grip. Polished marble can be slippery even when dry.

Energy plan

Ascent

Do not race the group. Use pauses as opportunities to look back across Athens.

Summit

Prioritize the Parthenon, Erechtheion and city panorama rather than circling repeatedly.

Plaka

Choose shade, water and a seated break before shopping or wandering.
