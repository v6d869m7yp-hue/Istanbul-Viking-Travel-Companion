National Archaeological Museum

This is a national collection, not a site museum. Use it to connect the Bronze Age world of Troy and Crete with the later development of Greek art.

Bronze Age
Mycenaean world
Gold, weapons, palace culture and elite burial.
Archaic
Experiment in the human figure
Kouroi and korai move toward greater naturalism.
Classical
Balance and motion
Bronzes and marble sculpture refine idealized anatomy.

Look for

  • How gold objects communicate rank and ritual
  • The transition from the “Archaic smile” toward natural expression
  • Bronze sculpture’s freedom from marble supports
  • Objects that connect trade routes across the eastern Mediterranean
