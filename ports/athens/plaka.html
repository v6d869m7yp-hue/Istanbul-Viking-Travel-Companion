Plaka: recover before you explore

Plaka is at its best when you stop treating it as another checklist. Use it for lunch, shade, atmosphere and a few carefully chosen lanes.

If energy is good

Take a short wander through quieter lanes, look back toward the Acropolis and browse selectively.

If energy is low

Sit down, hydrate and make the neighborhood itself the experience. There is no prize for maximizing distance.

Good sequence

1Find shade
2Cold drink
3Lunch or light meal
4One scenic lane
5Meeting point early
