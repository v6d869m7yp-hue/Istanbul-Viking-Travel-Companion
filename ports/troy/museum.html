The museum supplies the missing context

The site shows walls and layers; a museum can show objects, models, chronology and daily life. Whether your excursion includes it or only passes nearby, this guide tells you what matters most.

PriorityLook forWhy it matters
1Site models and layer diagramsThey make the mound intelligible before individual artifacts.
2Bronze Age ceramics and toolsThey connect monumental walls to ordinary life.
3Architectural fragmentsThey show the Greek and Roman reinvention of Troy.
4Excavation history displaysThey explain how interpretation changed after Schliemann.
If time is short: prioritize the large-scale models, chronology panels and objects tied to Troy VI–VII before browsing later Roman material.

A useful museum question

Ask: “Which objects were found in a secure archaeological context?” Provenience often matters more than visual drama.
