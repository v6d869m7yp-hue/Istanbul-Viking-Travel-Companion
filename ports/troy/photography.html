Photography Guide

How to photograph a layered archaeological site whose importance is not always obvious in a single frame.

Troy chapters

Photograph relationships, not isolated stones

Wide views, human scale and explanatory labels help photographs communicate Troy’s structure.

Replica horse

Shoot early before tour groups cluster; include enough background to establish place.

Defensive walls

Use a low angle to emphasize the slope and mass of the masonry.

Excavation trench

Frame multiple layers together; a person gives scale.

The plain

Include the landscape to suggest why the location mattered strategically.
