Istanbul–Viking Travel Companion v5.1.0

Shared Travel Vault collaboration foundation:
- richer reservation cards
- encrypted attachments up to 5 MB each
- activity timeline
- known-device registry
- encrypted collaboration packages for AirDrop, Messages, Mail or iCloud Drive
- merge rules that preserve conflicting edits
- explicit sync queue status

Automatic cloud sync, remote revocation and temporary public-PC sessions are not enabled until a secure backend is configured.
