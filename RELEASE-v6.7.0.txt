Istanbul–Viking Travel Companion v6.7.0
Secure Backup & Recovery

- First-run choice to create a new vault or restore an encrypted backup.
- Versioned encrypted backup envelope with SHA-256 integrity checking.
- Backup & Recovery dashboard with last-backup status and revision tracking.
- Backup reminder after secure documents, reservations, or traveler data change.
- Timestamped backup filenames so older generations are not overwritten.
- Restore accepts both v6.7 backup packages and legacy encrypted vault exports.
- Vault password remains separate from Firebase Authentication.
