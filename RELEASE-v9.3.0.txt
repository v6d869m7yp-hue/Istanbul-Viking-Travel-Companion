Istanbul–Viking Travel Companion v9.4.2 — Travel Dashboard
Release date: July 30, 2026

Highlights
- Replaces the static home briefing with a responsive day-of-travel dashboard.
- Shows pre-trip countdown, current trip day, or completed-trip status automatically.
- Combines itinerary and synchronized reservations to identify the next event.
- Adds a Today / departure-day agenda with times and travel details.
- Shows current or next hotel, vacation rental, or cruise stay with directions and details.
- Adds cloud/device-copy synchronization status.
- Adds trip-progress visualization using the complete reservation date range.
- Adds prominent quick actions for reservations, timeline, Vault documents, and maps.
- Supports direct “Add reservation” links from the dashboard.
- Preserves all v9.2 Smart Timeline and cross-device synchronization behavior.

Testing focus
1. Confirm the dashboard loads existing reservations immediately from the device copy.
2. Confirm signed-in cloud synchronization changes the status indicator to green.
3. Confirm Next Up matches the first upcoming itinerary or reservation event.
4. Confirm lodging shows the current stay during the trip or the next stay before departure.
5. Confirm Add reservation opens the unified reservation form.
6. Confirm the dashboard adapts correctly on iPhone, iPad, and desktop.
