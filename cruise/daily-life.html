Onboard rhythm

Life Onboard

A low-friction routine for busy port days and relaxed evenings.

Morning routine

Check the Viking Daily.
Confirm meeting time, meeting place, return time and any updated instructions.
Eat earlier than feels necessary.
Leave a buffer before the excursion so breakfast never becomes stressful.
Take only what the day needs.
Phone, water, hat, sunscreen, required documents and appropriate clothing.

After returning aboard

Recharge phone and camera
Refill water bottle
Put excursion items back in one place
Review tomorrow’s walking level
Check clothing requirements
Rest before dinner

Wi-Fi, laundry and dining

Shipboard services and operating details can change. Use the current onboard information as the authority. This companion’s role is to help you remember the questions to answer: where, when, whether a reservation is needed, and what must be ready before tomorrow.

Best habit: prepare tomorrow’s bag before dinner. The evening then belongs to you rather than to logistics.
