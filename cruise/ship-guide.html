Viking Vesta

Ship Essentials

A practical orientation based on how you will actually use the ship—not a room-by-room inventory.

Build a five-point mental map

1 · Home base

Your stateroom

Remember the nearest elevator or stairwell and which direction leads forward versus aft.

2 · Morning

Breakfast and coffee

Choose one dependable breakfast location and one quieter coffee stop for early port days.

3 · Information

Guest services and excursion meeting

Know where to ask questions and where excursion groups assemble.

4 · Recovery

Quiet lounge or outdoor seat

Identify a comfortable place to decompress after a long excursion.

5 · Evening

Dinner and tomorrow’s briefing

End each day by reviewing the Viking Daily and this companion’s port preview.

Safety

Medical and emergency information

Note the current shipboard instructions and where emergency information is posted.

Stateroom setup

Use the safe for passports
Keep medications together
Create a charging station
Use one drawer for excursion gear
Keep tomorrow’s clothes visible
Store paper schedules in one place
Do not over-organize on the first day. A simple, repeatable system is more useful than unpacking every item perfectly.
