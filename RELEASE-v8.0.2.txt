Version 8.1.1 — Firestore Vault Bridge

- Adds a Safari-compatible encrypted Vault transport through chunked Firestore documents.
- Mac sync now publishes the same encrypted Vault snapshot to Firebase Storage and the Firestore bridge.
- iPad restore tries the Firestore bridge first and falls back to Storage.
- No password or decrypted Vault contents are written to Firebase.
- After deployment, open the Mac Vault and run Sync now once before restoring on iPad.
