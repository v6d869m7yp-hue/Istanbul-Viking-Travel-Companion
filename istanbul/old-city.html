1
Morning

Blue Mosque

Enter respectfully and allow for prayer-related access changes. Outside, step back into the park to understand the cascade of domes and six minarets.

Look for: İznik tilework, filtered window light and the relationship between the central dome and supporting semi-domes.
2
Roman Constantinople

Hippodrome

Walk the long axis slowly. The open square conceals the scale of the former chariot-racing arena and political gathering place.

Look for: the surviving monuments along the spina and the difference between today's ground level and the ancient structure.
3
Byzantine masterpiece

Hagia Sophia

Give your eyes time to adjust. The experience is less about individual decoration than the extraordinary volume beneath the dome and the building's layered Christian and Islamic history.

Look for: marble revetment, giant calligraphic roundels, surviving mosaics where visible and the apparent weightlessness of the dome.
4
Cool midday interlude

Basilica Cistern

A good contrast after the exposed square. Move beyond the first photo cluster before stopping; the deeper views often feel more atmospheric.

Look for: reused column capitals, reflected rows and the Medusa-head bases.
5
Afternoon choice

Topkapı Palace—or a slower finish

Topkapı merits several hours, especially with the Harem. Choose it only if energy remains. The alternative is lunch, the Archaeological Museums area, Gülhane Park and an earlier rooftop rest.

Best pacing decision: The palace is a destination, not a box to check. Move it to August 17 if the morning sites run long.
