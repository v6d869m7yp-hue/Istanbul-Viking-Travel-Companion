Istanbul / Food
Eating well

Choose meals by moment, not fame

The best restaurant for arrival night is not the best restaurant for a long Bosphorus lunch.

01

Arrival dinner

Nearby, calm and reservation-light. Favor a terrace or courtyard over a cross-city destination.

02

Old City lunch

Efficient but seated. A 60-minute reset will improve the palace or museum afternoon.

03

Bosphorus meal

Prioritize water views or neighborhood character; allow extra time for ferry connections.

04

Final-night dinner

The right time for a more polished meal, rooftop sunset or destination restaurant.

What to try

A practical tasting list

Turkish breakfast

Cheese, olives, tomato, cucumber, eggs, bread, preserves and tea.

Meze

Small cold and warm plates; ideal when you want variety without a heavy main course.

Kebabs & grills

Look beyond the generic mixed grill and ask about the house regional specialty.

Fish

Best enjoyed with transparent pricing; confirm the fish and price before ordering.

Baklava

Try pistachio and walnut versions; freshness matters more than elaborate presentation.

Turkish coffee

Small, strong and unfiltered; let the grounds settle before the final sip.

Restaurant intelligence

Quick quality checks

  • Menus display prices clearly
  • Staff explain fish or market-price items before ordering
  • The restaurant is busy with a mix of visitors and locals
  • Reviews mention food and service—not only the view
  • You are not pressured by a street tout
