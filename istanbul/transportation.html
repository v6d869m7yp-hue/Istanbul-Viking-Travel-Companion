Core principle

Walk locally; use rails and ferries for distance

Your hotel location makes the Old City largely walkable. The tram is the most useful surface connection, while ferries turn transportation into sightseeing.

Old City

Walking

Best for Sultanahmet sights. Expect uneven stone, crowds and occasional slopes; use supportive shoes.

Across the center

Tram

Useful between Sultanahmet, Eminönü and the Galata Bridge area. Keep your transit card accessible before reaching the gate.

Europe and Asia

Ferries

The most enjoyable way to cross the Bosphorus. Arrive a little early so boarding does not feel rushed.

Door to door

Taxi

Best when luggage, heat or limited walking makes public transport inconvenient. Ask the hotel to arrange one when possible.

Airport

Private transfer

For arrival after a long flight, a prearranged transfer reduces decisions and keeps the first day calm.

Navigation

Offline backup

Save the hotel address and a map screenshot before leaving Wi-Fi. Carry the hotel card as a non-digital backup.
