The essential idea

Byzantium → Constantinople → Istanbul

The city changed names, rulers and religions, but its strategic position on the Bosphorus kept it at the center of trade and empire.

Ancient

Byzantium

A Greek settlement established at a naturally defensible and commercially powerful point between the Black Sea and Mediterranean worlds.

Roman & Byzantine

Constantinople

Refounded as an imperial capital, the city became the eastern center of the Roman world. Hagia Sophia embodies this era's scale and ambition.

Ottoman

Imperial Istanbul

After the Ottoman conquest, existing monuments were adapted and new mosques, palaces, markets and civic institutions reshaped the skyline.

Modern

A living metropolis

The historic peninsula remains only one part of a vast contemporary city whose neighborhoods stretch across both Europe and Asia.
