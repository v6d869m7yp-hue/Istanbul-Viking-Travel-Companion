Four days without rushing Istanbul

FRI14AUG
Arrival day

Check in, orient and enjoy Sultanahmet at dusk

Priority: recover from travel. Walk from the hotel toward the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia, have an early nearby dinner and return before fatigue catches up.

Late afternoon

Hotel check-in, unpack essentials, verify next day's opening plans.

Evening

Short Sultanahmet loop; no ticketed attraction required.

SAT15AUG
Imperial Istanbul

Blue Mosque, Hippodrome, Hagia Sophia and one major paid site

Use the morning for the mosque district. After lunch, choose the Basilica Cistern and either Topkapı Palace or a slower museum/park afternoon. Avoid forcing both palace and every museum into the same day.

Open the complete Old City route →
SUN16AUG
The water city

Spice Bazaar, public ferry and Bosphorus neighborhoods

Start around Eminönü, then let the ferry become part of the sightseeing. Choose either an Asian-side stop or an Ortaköy/Dolmabahçe combination, not both unless energy is high.

Open the Bosphorus day →
MON17AUG
Flexible final full day

Grand Bazaar plus the experience you most want to deepen

Reserve this day for the Grand Bazaar, a quieter Ottoman neighborhood, the Asian side, a museum missed earlier or simple unstructured wandering. This buffer protects the trip from weather, closures and fatigue.

TUE18AUG
Embarkation

Breakfast, final walk and transfer to Viking

Keep passports, medicine, electronics and boarding documents in your carry-on. Leave generous traffic time and treat the ship—not another major sight—as today's destination.

Planning note: Mosque access, attraction hours and ticket procedures can change. Recheck official information shortly before each visit and keep the flexible day available for adjustments.
