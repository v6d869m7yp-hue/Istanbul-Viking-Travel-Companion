Field guide

What to notice before anything else

1 · The dome

Stand back and read how the central dome seems to float on light-filled pendentives rather than sitting heavily on walls.

2 · The marble

Look down as well as up. Book-matched marble panels and worn thresholds preserve the physical passage of centuries.

3 · Layered symbols

Christian mosaics, Islamic calligraphy and architectural adaptations occupy the same enormous volume.

4 · Scale

Pause near a human figure or doorway to recalibrate the apparent size of the interior.

Byzantine → Ottoman → modern timeline

537Imperial church completed

Justinian’s architects create an unprecedented domed interior.

1453Converted to an imperial mosque

Minarets, mihrab and other Islamic elements develop around the Byzantine fabric.

1935Museum era

The building becomes a secular museum and mosaics receive renewed attention.

2020Mosque use resumes

Visitor access now coexists with active worship; operational arrangements can change.

Photography guide

  • Exterior: frame from Sultanahmet Square with foreground trees or fountains
  • Interior: expose for highlights around windows and let shadows remain atmospheric
  • Details: marble panels, capitals, roundels and threshold wear
  • Respect worship areas and current photography restrictions
