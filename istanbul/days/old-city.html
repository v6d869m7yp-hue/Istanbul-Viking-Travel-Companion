Travel intelligence

Carry before leaving

  • Water and sun protection
  • Comfortable shoes with grip
  • Light scarf or modest layer

Energy-saving shortcut: When Topkapı feels too ambitious after the morning monuments, replace it with Gülhane Park and a long café stop. The day will still feel complete.
What most people miss

The Hippodrome is not empty space

The landscaped axis between the monuments follows the ancient chariot-racing arena. Read the surviving obelisks and columns as markers of a vast public space that once shaped imperial politics.
