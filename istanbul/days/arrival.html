Independent Istanbul

Day 1 · Arrival & First Evening

A gentle first evening that respects jet lag.

Arrival sequence

Clear immigration, collect luggage, complete the hotel transfer and settle in before attempting sightseeing.

Best first walk: Dersaadet Hotel → Blue Mosque exterior → Sultanahmet Square → Hagia Sophia exterior → hotel.

Evening objective

Orient yourself, eat close to the hotel, and protect the following day from overambitious arrival-night plans.
