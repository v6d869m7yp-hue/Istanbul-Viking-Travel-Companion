Istanbul / Practical
Pocket guide

The details that keep days smooth

Transit, etiquette, walking, payments and a few useful words.

Transit

Trams and ferries are often faster and more scenic than road travel. Keep a contactless transit strategy ready and avoid planning tight connections around rush-hour driving.

Walking

Expect uneven paving, polished stone and hills outside the central monument zone. Supportive shoes matter more than dressy shoes.

Mosques

Dress modestly, remove shoes where directed, keep voices low and accept that visitor access may pause around prayer.

Money

Cards are widely useful, but carry modest local cash for small purchases. Prefer bank-affiliated ATMs and decline unnecessary dynamic currency conversion when offered.

Taxis

Use an official or app-arranged taxi, confirm the destination, and keep the hotel address pin available. Traffic—not distance—often determines the ride.

Heat

August can make exposed squares tiring. Start early, seek shade, carry water and make lunch a cooling break.

Avoidable problems

Pause before agreeing

  • Unsolicited “help” with transport or tickets
  • Restaurant invitations without visible menus and prices
  • Shoe-shine or dropped-object distractions
  • Overpacked daily schedules with no traffic buffer
  • Carrying original documents when a secure alternative is appropriate
