Istanbul–Viking Travel Companion v6.4.1 — Map Link Repair

Fixes a v6.4.0 regression that prevented interactive map activation on pages whose SVG maps use data-map-zoom or map-panel markup instead of the older zoomable-map/hotspot-map classes.

- Supports all map markup patterns used in the project.
- Initializes correctly even when the script loads after DOMContentLoaded.
- Preserves v6.4.0 My Trips and Firebase functionality.
