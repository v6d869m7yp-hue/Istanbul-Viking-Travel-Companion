v8.0.4 — Offline Trip Recovery

- Prevents My Trips from hanging indefinitely when Firestore network reads stall.
- Reads cached trips first and falls back to them after a bounded cloud timeout.
- Creates the packaged Istanbul trip with a deterministic per-user ID.
- Makes the trip immediately active locally while its Firestore write is queued.
- Preserves the six locally available reservation records until cloud synchronization resumes.
