Istanbul–Viking Travel Companion v9.5.1 — Smart Timeline
Release date: July 30, 2026

Highlights
- Combines the curated trip itinerary with synchronized reservations in one chronological timeline.
- Automatically creates separate check-in and check-out timeline events for hotels, rentals and cruise stays.
- Includes flights, trains, restaurants, transportation, activities and other reservation types.
- Adds All, Itinerary, Travel, Lodging, Dining and Activities filters with live counts.
- Shows a Today marker during the trip.
- Adds Directions, Call, Website and Reservation Details actions to reservation timeline cards.
- Loads the device copy first, then upgrades to live cloud synchronization after sign-in.
- Preserves the existing reservation collection, offline shadow and Firebase rules.
